# Martin Etzrodt

Director of the [Open Science Institute](https://opening.science/institute/) in Interlaken, the research arm of the Swiss Open Science Foundation, which funds and governs it. I work on open and collaborative science: open hardware, open health and open infrastructure.

## What I build and contribute to

- **Open-hardware biophoton microscope**, built with [openUC2](https://openuc2.com) in Jena. First measurement campaign June 2026. [Results](https://opening.science/news/biophoton-microscope-jena/)
- **Biophoton / Ultra-Weak Photon Emission Field Map**: 18,355 works and 39,312 authors, scored for openness. [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21466493](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21466493)
- **Open Biocenter**, a remotely operated biology platform hosted at the institute. [biocenter.opening.science](https://biocenter.opening.science) · [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22685449](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22685449)
- **OpenTwin XR** and the **open-twin** FHIR connectors for open digital-twin tooling. [Opening-Science](https://github.com/Opening-Science)
- **Conjecture**, an open hypothesis hub, with its biophoton domain pack.

## Background

I am a biochemist by training and worked as an immunologist and stem cell scientist through my PhD and postdoc. I got my PhD from the Université de Lausanne, with doctoral research at Harvard Medical School, and did postdoctoral work at Helmholtz Zentrum München and ETH Zürich. In a 2018 essay ([DOI 10.5281/zenodo.1156360](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1156360)) I introduced the idea of decentralizing science and set out my vision of how open science should evolve on the web. I chair the ISCC Foundation.
